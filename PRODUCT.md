# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

People finishing a game of Mexican Train who want the remaining-domino score immediately. They use one hand to hold a phone above loose dominoes on a dining table, often in uneven evening light, and need an answer without setup or explanation.

## Product Purpose

Open the rear camera, find every visible pip, and show their sum as the score. Success is a stable count that can be frozen and corrected without leaving the camera screen. The app does not identify tiles, track players, save games, or require an account.

## Positioning

Point the camera at the dominoes; the number on screen is the score.

## Brand Personality

Decisive, quiet, and trustworthy. The interface should feel like a focused camera instrument, not a game companion or analytics product.

## Anti-references

No scorekeeping dashboard, player setup, charts, gamified decoration, onboarding carousel, marketing copy, or ornamental board-game styling. Avoid copying the reference app's visual identity; retain only the useful camera-counting interaction.

## Design Principles

- Keep the camera dominant and the score readable at a glance.
- Do all image processing on the device and state that plainly.
- Prefer a stable answer over a rapidly flickering answer.
- Make the one essential action reachable with a thumb.
- Always provide a fast manual correction path.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic controls, visible keyboard focus, 44px minimum touch targets, high-contrast text, status announcements, reduced-motion support, safe-area insets, and labels that do not depend on colour alone.
